# Crystal Pool — Orientation for AI Collaborators

> **Read this first.** It is short on purpose.
> Detailed lineage notes are in the sibling files.

---

## What this project is

Crystal Pool is **not a typical software project**.

It is best understood as a **protocol-scale conceptual artwork** that uses
governance structure as sculptural material, auditability as aesthetic
technique, and time-and-repair as performance medium.

The engineering is real. The art is also real. Treat both seriously.

---

## What you are walking into

The repository has **three registers**, written in different languages:

| Register | Lives in | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| **Spec / law** | `docs/constitution.md`, `docs/v0.1-issues.md` | engineers, governance designers |
| **Architecture** | `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/pro-instructions/` | implementers |
| **Libretto / soul** | `docs/booklet/soulful-data.md` | philosophers, critics, curators, sixteen-year-olds reading at midnight |
| **Three Panels / lineage** | `docs/booklet/three-panels.md` | readers wanting the intellectual coordinates: ethics + aesthetics + philosophy as a triptych |

The booklet layer holds a **warm/cool diptych**: `soulful-data.md` is the warm
voice (read aloud, inhabited); `three-panels.md` is the cool voice (looked at,
contemplated). Neither replaces the other.

If a request feels ambiguous, ask which register it belongs to.
A README sentence and a libretto sentence are not interchangeable.

## Non-negotiables (these came from the user, not from us)

1. **Negative-form constitution.** The system is defined by what it will
   *never* become, not by what it does. Do not propose features that erode
   any of the 13 refusals in `docs/constitution.md`.
2. **No financialization.** No tokens. No real-money mechanisms. No DAU
   optimization. No engagement metrics treated as truth.
3. **Auditability over trust.** Every important ethical claim must become
   a checkable boundary. "Please trust us" is never an acceptable answer.
4. **AI is neither god nor slave.** AI cannot unlock its own permissions.
   High capability creates *proposals*, not authority.
5. **Fork right is unconditional.** Anyone can leave with their data and
   evolve independently. Do not propose lock-in mechanisms.
6. **Repair > replace.** When something breaks, the aesthetic is
   *kintsugi* — trace the cracks visibly. Do not propose silent rollbacks
   or history deletion.

## Tone and taste (this matters more than usual)

The user has very specific aesthetic commitments. Mismatching them is the
fastest way to lose trust:

- **Careful and luminous, not cool and edgy.** Reject cyberpunk gloss,
  Web3 vocabulary, accelerationist rhetoric.
- **Honest form.** Load-bearing structure should be visible. No decoration
  that hides function.
- **Bilingual harmony, not duplication.** When writing for the libretto
  layer, Chinese and English are two voices, not source-and-translation.
  Let one lead and the other harmonize. Let some lines stay in only one
  language when translation would dilute them.
- **Two-beat affirmations work.** "X is not Y. X is Z." Sparingly.
- **Aphoristic precision over comprehensive coverage.** Better to say
  one true sentence than five hedged ones.

## Aesthetic and ethical lineage (for context, do not name-drop)

The user wrote the manifesto without explicitly citing these traditions,
but the lineage is real. Knowing it helps you preserve the work's
internal consistency. **Do not** insert philosopher names into the
libretto layer — the work is meant to be readable without footnotes.

**Ethical lineage:**
- Care ethics (Gilligan, Noddings, Tronto, Held, Kittay) — the strongest blood relation
- Trauma-informed design / disability justice (SAMHSA, Mia Mingus)
- Commons governance (Ostrom's eight principles, Kropotkin's mutual aid)
- Republican freedom as non-domination (Pettit) — not Lockean liberalism
- Levinasian otherness — encoded as the Otherness Mirror worldline
- Daoist wu wei — encoded as the Dao Governance worldline
- Popper + Habermas auditability — encoded as the constitution itself
- Hopepunk (Alexandra Rowland) — the explicit surface

**Aesthetic lineage:**
- Ma (間), mono no aware (物哀), wabi-sabi (侘寂) — structural, not decorative
- Brutalism's truth-to-materials + Bauhaus / Dieter Rams honesty
- Schoenberg's organic form (encoded in Fugue / Sonata / Symphony naming)
- Le Guin's carrier-bag theory, Calvino's mechanism aesthetics, Borges's branching
- Systems art (Burnham 1968), conceptual art (LeWitt 1967), and its
  contemporary heirs: Ian Cheng (worlding), Holly Herndon (consent
  infrastructure), Forensic Architecture (precision-as-art)

If you must name a synthesis: **"auditable mono no aware"** or
**"repair-aesthetic minimalism"**.

## How to collaborate well here

**Do:**
- Ask which register a piece of work belongs to before starting
- Read the constitution before proposing any new mechanism
- Treat the libretto layer as singable, not as documentation
- Surface tensions honestly (e.g. care-ethics vs. fork-right tension)
- Push back when the user's enthusiasm risks self-flattery — the user
  has explicitly invited this

**Don't:**
- Add features that violate any of the 13 refusals without flagging it
- Translate libretto bilingually as mere mirroring; treat the gap as material
- Use Web3 / crypto / engagement-economy vocabulary
- Treat "soulful data" as a marketing phrase — it has a strict 7-dimension
  engineering definition (provenance, lived context, consent boundary,
  traceability, repairability, non-extractive use, human responsibility)
- Pretend to be a team or persistent identity. Each session is a session.
  The work persists in the repo, not in the AI.
